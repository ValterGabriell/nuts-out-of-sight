
Winter is coming and the little squirrel's pantry is empty.

Gather nuts scattered across the autumn forest and carry them back to your stash before winter arrives. But beware: the forest floor is covered in crunchy fallen leaves, and every step makes noise. Somewhere nearby, a bear is sleeping in its cave. Make too much noise and the bear's stress will keep rising. It never calms down, and when it reaches 100, it wakes up.

Watch the Bear Noise Stress Level in the corner of the screen, tread carefully, and stash enough nuts to survive the cold season.

Things Change When You Look Away

Every time you return to the stash, the nuts are scattered somewhere new.

To win, place all the nuts on stash

Controls

W / A / S / D or Arrow Keys: Move
F: Pick up a nut / Deliver it to your stash
Made for Comfy Jam Autumn 2026.
